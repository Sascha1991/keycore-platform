# Visible Storefront Human UAT - 2026-08-30

## Decision

The product owner accepted the tested visible-storefront scope on 2026-08-30.
This is a scoped human result, not complete KS-11-07 approval. The recorded UTC
timestamp uses midnight as a date-only normalization because the supplied human
record contained the review date but no execution time.

## Accepted Scope

- The Germany-first KeyRaNo storefront was visible with the corrected German
  presentation and six synthetic products.
- Product detail, cart and the checkout shell worked. Checkout exposed no live
  payment method and no payment was submitted.
- The local HTTP staging path no longer produced the incorrect HTTPS redirect.
- Customer A could see the owned synthetic `READY` purchase.
- No Product Key was visible before the explicit reveal action.
- Secure reveal displayed only the approved synthetic staging key. The value is
  deliberately omitted from this record.
- Customer B received `Dieser Kauf ist nicht verfügbar.` when directly opening
  customer A's purchase. Cross-owner detail, reveal and key access remained
  blocked.

These observations complete UAT-001 and UAT-006 for the approved synthetic
staging fixture. The tested account-list/detail and checkout-shell portions are
accepted observations, but do not complete UAT-002, UAT-015 or UAT-018.
