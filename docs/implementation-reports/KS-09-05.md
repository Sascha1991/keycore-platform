## Architecture

- `SupplierClaim` is an internal provider-neutral aggregate.
- `SupplierClaimAuthorityPort` defaults to fail-closed denial.
- `SupplierClaimSubmissionPort` defaults to unavailable.
- Internal claim state is separate from durable external submission state.
- Supplier identity and supplier order reference derive from persisted
  procurement, never request authority.
- Customer assertions remain reported classifications and are not converted to
  verified supplier fault.
- PostgreSQL is the durable source of truth.

## Eligibility and Evidence

- Claims require a real order, eligible order-scoped support case and exact
  procurement operation.
- Normal claims require confirmed successful procurement.
- Ambiguous/reconciliation-required procurement is represented only as
  `SUPPLIER_ORDER_PROBLEM` and does not imply purchase success.
- Key-related categories require exact-order, exact-procurement fulfillment
  metadata without reading fulfillment secrets.
- Only finalized, exact-order dispute evidence can link.
- Customer message bodies, internal notes and evidence JSON are not copied.

Tables:

Database protections include enum/tuple/time/version checks, foreign keys,
unique idempotency, one active exact-issue claim, immutable identity,
procurement-derived supplier identity, exact-order triggers, finalized evidence
checks and append-only event/link triggers. Claim/event, transition/event,
evidence/event and submission/event writes are transactional.
Evidence is frozen after submission preparation, and PostgreSQL rejects direct
submission state skips.

## Submission Semantics

`READY_FOR_SUBMISSION` is internal readiness only. Submission operations use
`PREPARED`, `DISPATCHING`, `CONFIRMED`, `AMBIGUOUS` and `FAILED`. A dispatch
exception becomes `AMBIGUOUS`; automatic redispatch is blocked. A future
adapter must reconcile authoritative supplier state and use stable idempotency
references for effectively-exactly-once business effect.

The repository's represented official Kinguin documentation does not verify a
generic claim endpoint. The documented key-return mutation is not used as a
claim workflow. Production submission remains unavailable.
