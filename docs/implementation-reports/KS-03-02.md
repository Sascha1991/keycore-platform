## Architecture

The implementation adds `SupplierRoutingService`, `ProductSupplierMappingPort`, `RegionEligibilityPort`, optional `CurrencyConversionPort`, candidate models, routing policy models and fallback-plan models.

Routing evaluates explicit canonical-product to supplier-offer mappings. Supplier-specific IDs remain scoped to each supplier, and title matching is not used.

## Eligibility And Selection

Candidates are rejected fail-closed for unsafe states including disabled or draining suppliers, missing offers, unsupported capabilities, out-of-stock or unknown availability, stale prices, outage or unknown health when disallowed, exhausted rate limits, unsupported currency, blocked or unclear region evidence, VPN requirement and foreign-account requirement.

Eligible suppliers are ranked by health, availability, comparable price, configured supplier priority, supplier ID and supplier offer ID.

## Fallback Safety

Fallback planning is fail-closed by purchase-attempt state:

- `FAILED_TERMINAL` may exclude that supplier and return the next untouched eligible supplier.
- `AMBIGUOUS` returns `RECONCILE_CURRENT_SUPPLIER_FIRST` with no candidates.
- `FAILED_RETRYABLE` returns `NO_FALLBACK` with no candidates.
- `SUCCEEDED` returns `NO_FALLBACK` with no candidates.
- `NOT_STARTED` is not treated as a failed purchase and does not trigger cross-supplier fallback.

Conflicting attempts choose the safest state: ambiguous requires reconciliation, succeeded prevents further purchase and retryable prevents automatic cross-supplier switching.
